# Prompt ilustrasi AMANA

Dibuat memakai built-in image_gen; transparent_background=true pada semua aset. Versi web dikodekan ke WebP tanpa mengubah dimensi kanvas.

## Bapak penjual gerobak

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a production-quality isolated illustration asset for the AMANA website and presentation slides, NOT a website mockup, chart, diagram, infographic, icon sheet or presentation layout.
Shared art direction for the entire AMANA asset family: distinctive contemporary 2D editorial cartoon, clean confident forest-green ink contours, attractive rounded but anatomically believable forms, broad matte flat color areas, tiny restrained secondary shadows, human warmth and careful drawing. A professional Indonesian startup visual language, approachable and polished, not infantile. Consistent palette: deep emerald #075541, forest #18372D, sage #CBDDD0, warm cream #F7F8F4; small natural ochre/tan accents and warm natural skin tones. Do not tint skin green. No shiny 3D, clay, emoji style, generic AI glow or stock clip-art gradients.
Composition: the requested subject forms one cohesive, self-contained illustrated scene, fully visible, with 10% transparent breathing room around its complete silhouette. Full objects and full relevant limbs, no cropping at canvas edges. A wide or square composition appropriate for reuse at 400–800px on a landing page. True transparent background and clean alpha edges; no opaque white rectangle, checkerboard pattern, colored backdrop, decorative card, circular disk, border or presentation frame. No text, lettering, numbers, brand logos, fake legal seals, watermark, chart axes or arrows.

Subject: one friendly middle-aged Indonesian male street-food entrepreneur beside his modest pushcart, with gentle smile and a neat small moustache, cream short-sleeved shirt, deep emerald apron, dark trousers and simple sandals. His hand rests naturally on the cart's handle. The food cart has two substantial functional wheels, a pale-sage awning, a modest glass food compartment with a pot and two bowls, and an emerald lower cabinet. Human and cart should have thoughtful believable proportions. Show the man's full body, the whole cart and all wheels. 3:2 landscape scene, merchant on the left and cart on the right, grouped tightly enough to be a useful website cutout. No signage or words. This is the first style anchor for a coherent family of eight illustrations.
```
